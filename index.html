Before you reserve

Common questions

How much is the reservation fee?

₱100,000 for the RFO houses. For lots it is ₱75,000 or ₱100,000, depending on the lot, as printed on the price list. The reservation fee is non-refundable and non-transferable.

What is included in the list price?

The list price includes 12% VAT, registration and miscellaneous fees such as documentary stamp tax, transfer tax and other fees for the transfer of the title (TCT).

What if the bank does not approve my loan?

Per the developer's price list, in-house financing automatically applies if a buyer is not approved for bank financing. Ask me for the in-house terms.

Can I pay from abroad?

Yes. Transactions are in Philippine pesos; dollar amounts are estimates only. Message me and I'll walk you through payment arrangements for buyers abroad.

Who do I pay?

All payments are made payable only to Crown Asia Properties, Inc., the developer on the official price lists and computations.

Which enclave is right for me?

Belize has the RFO houses and the most affordable lots. Amalfi has Mediterranean-style streets. Caribé has the biggest lots and the most amenities. Tell me your budget and plans and I'll shortlist for you.

Can I visit the houses and lots?

Yes. Request a site viewing below and I'll confirm the schedule with you first.
